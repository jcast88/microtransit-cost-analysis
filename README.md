Microtransit Cost Analysis

This repository contains R scripts used to evaluate the performance and cost-effectiveness of microtransit services operated by Circuit and Via in the San Diego region.

Overview

The analysis uses trip-level ridership data, service schedules, and operating cost assumptions to evaluate service performance and identify opportunities to improve efficiency.

Key metrics include:

  Ridership: Daily and monthly ridership trends, trip volumes, and passenger counts.

  Service Performance: Ridership by day of week and time of day, trip characteristics, wait times, and vehicle occupancy.

  Operating Costs: Contract costs, cost per passenger, and riders per vehicle revenue hour.

  Service Efficiency: Pooling, vehicle utilization, estimated passenger capacity, and scenarios for achieving cost-efficiency targets.

Analysis Scripts

  Via Analysis: Evaluates the El Cajon and Southeastern San Diego microtransit pilots, including ridership patterns, service characteristics, contract costs, pooling, and capacity scenarios.

  Circuit Analysis: Evaluates Circuit microtransit pilots for FRED, PB Beach Bug, and Oceanside, focusing on ridership, service productivity, operating costs, and efficiency.

Data and Outputs

Scripts use service-provider trip data and operating assumptions to produce summary tables and Excel workbooks for reporting and further analysis.
